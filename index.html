Welcome to Moonroot Valley

Your little farm sits on the edge of a very hungry forest. Grow a harvest in daylight, then use your earnings to keep the creatures away after dark.

  • Day: Choose a seed and click an empty garden bed. Crops grow while you play; click a ripe one to sell it.
  • Night: Place defenses on the stone pads. They automatically fire at anything on the road.
  • Build: Select a tower, then click an empty stone pad. Towers persist between nights.
  • Survive: Protect your homestead through each wave. A new morning brings a small repair and a fresh crop of opportunities.

Tip: Turnips grow quickly. Berries and moonflowers take longer, but reward more gold.
